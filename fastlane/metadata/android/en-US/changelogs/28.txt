Magazines and newspapers: hold a folder of the library long to follow it; it then sits at the top of the library with the number of new issues, and one tap fetches them and opens the new one. Nothing is downloaded by itself. Several issues of one magazine make one line on the phone's shelf. The contents of an issue are whole pages, turned like the book's pages, with larger pictures; inside an article, a bar on top leads to the previous article, the contents and the next one, and every article reopens where you left it. Italic and bold now show in every book.
