Books and magazines are set the way their publisher set them: headings in their sizes, centred or right-aligned lines, justified text, indents and the space between paragraphs, quotations and verse in their margins, lists with their bullets and numbers, tables as grids, small capitals, notes raised, rules, pictures at their own size with their captions, large initials, a new page where the book asks for one, line breaks of code and verse. Your font, size, margins and colours stay yours. Text written over several lines of the source now reads as one paragraph.
