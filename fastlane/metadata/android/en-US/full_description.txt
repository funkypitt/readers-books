A black-and-white e-book reader, text only, made for e-ink phones and for anyone who wants a page and nothing else. No icons, no colours, no wallpaper: white on black or black on white, serif, sans or mono, three text sizes. Part of the Reader's family of apps (Reader's Launcher, Notes, Tasks, Calendar…).

The shelf is a list: title, how far you are, when you last opened it. Tap to read. The page is plain text, cut at whole lines, never through a line: the right half of the screen turns forward, the left half back, a long press opens the menu (chapters, larger or smaller text, back to the shelf, remove). The reading position is remembered per book and survives a change of text size or screen. The screen stays on while a page is open.

Formats: EPUB, MOBI (PalmDoc), FB2 and plain text. Images, styles and footnote links are dropped; only the text remains. Open a book from the shelf, from a file manager ("open with"), or by sharing the file to the app. Books are copied into the app, so the original can move.

A standard home-screen widget for any launcher shows the book being read; a tap carries on at the current page. Reader's Launcher shows the same on its book tile.

No network, no account, no tracking. English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian. Free software, MIT licence.
